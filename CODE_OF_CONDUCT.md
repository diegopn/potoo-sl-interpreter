# Code of Conduct

The SL project aims to maintain an open, respectful, and constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and consider differences in background, experience, and perspective.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, and give and receive it constructively.
- Respect personal boundaries and privacy, and credit other people's work.
- Take responsibility for the impact of your actions and help repair harm when it occurs.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Personal attacks, insults, slurs, or unwelcome sexualized language or attention.
- Sharing private or confidential information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project spaces or retaliating against someone who raises a concern.

## Scope

This code applies to this repository and project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, comments, and documentation. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness conduct that violates this code, contact the maintainer through the [Potoo contact page](https://diegopn.github.io/#contato) to request a private channel. Do not post incident details in a public issue, pull request, or discussion, or include sensitive details in the initial contact.

Reports will be reviewed as soon as practical and handled discreetly. The maintainer may clarify and apply these standards proportionately, including by removing content or limiting participation.
